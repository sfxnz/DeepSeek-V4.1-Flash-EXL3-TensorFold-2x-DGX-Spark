0. Bug found and fixed (b5409b7, fix(deepseek_v41): capture stopped chains on a high-priority stream)
  torch 2.13's begin_capture_to_if_node captures each body on the next stream of the low-priority pool (32, round robin).
  torch.cuda.graph's capture stream comes from the same pool, so after at most 31 bodies in a capture the pool hands
  back the capturing stream itself and cudaStreamBeginCaptureToGraph fails with cudaErrorIllegalState. A chain at d=5
  holds 16 bodies, so every Graphs.warm with the stop failed (first suite run: 25 golden errors + 2 fails knob off,
  142 errors + 6 fails knob on, all this error). Repro: pool_repro/repro.out (default capture stream fails at if-node
  31; a high-priority capture stream holds 80). Fix: Stop.stream (priority -1, never handed out by the low pool);
  Graphs._capture takes a stream and the chain graphs use it when the stop is on. New GPU test
  test_a_capture_on_the_stop_stream_holds_more_bodies_than_the_stream_pool (80 bodies; fails before: no Stop.stream).
  Two test-side changes for knob-on runs (f8f2aa2, 16d4b7c): test_graphs_replay_the_eager_rounds and dsv41_tp_check
  hashed the whole dbuf.dlog after a proposal; a stopped graph leaves the rows after the kept drafts without their
  Markov bias (never read by the host), so with the stop on they now hash dlog[:len(drafts)]. Knob off: unchanged.
